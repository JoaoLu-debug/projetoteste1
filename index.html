ABOUT

OUR METHODOLOGY

Studio Narrative operates at the intersection of travel curation and cinematic film production. We view landscape routes not just as destinations, but as structured storyboards waiting to be documented.

Our process merges detailed logistical travel planning with traditional film editing methods. Using 16mm analog film, natural audio recordings, and geographic metadata, we build immersive route guides that serve as tools for physical and visual exploration.

ROUTE ANALYSIS We compile coordinate guides, terrain elevation mapping, and historical context briefs for each path.
CINEMATIC ARCHIVE Every journey is captured on real analog film, focusing on composition, natural lighting, and zero artificial rendering.
